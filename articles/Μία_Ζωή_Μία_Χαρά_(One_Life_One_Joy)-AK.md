# Handover Document

## "Μία Ζωή, Μία Χαρά" (One Life, One Joy)
### Project Brief & Production Pack

---

**Date:** August 2026
**Project Type:** Educational / Cultural Folk Song
**Target Release:** Schools, Radio, Streaming Platforms, Tourism Promotion
**Duration (Full):** ≈ 6:30 | **(Radio Edit):** ≈ 3:00 | **(School Edit):** ≈ 3:30

---

## 1. Project Overview

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Title** | *Μία Ζωή, Μία Χαρά* (One Life, One Joy) |
| **Concept** | A cumulative Greek learning song in the style of "12 Days of Christmas" – teaching numbers 1–14, all 7 days of the week, 14 key nouns, and 10+ verbs through joyful repetition. |
| **Core Purpose** | To teach Greek language naturally while embedding subtle life lessons (gratitude, community, generosity, love) – universally accessible, culturally authentic, and spiritually resonant without being religious. |
| **Vibe** | Warm, sunlit, slightly playful, taverna-singalong, child-friendly but adult-enjoyable. |

---

## 2. Structural Blueprint

| Section | Content | Function |
|---------|---------|----------|
| **Intro** (Spoken) | Counting 1–14 over bouzouki intro | Warm-up, numbers drill |
| **Chorus** | *"Μία είναι η ζωή μας..."* | Anchor – repeated after every verse (15+ times). The "1 thing" – life is beautiful with love. |
| **Verses 1–7** (Week 1) | Sun → Seas → Islands → Friends → Fruit → Songs → Neighbourhoods | Concrete nouns, gifts of creation & community. Builds gratitude. |
| **Bridge 1** | *"Σταμάτα μια στιγμή..."* | Pause, reflection, count blessings. Slows down for emotional reset. |
| **Verses 8–14** (Week 2) | Coffee → Kisses → Parties → Stories → Meals → Moons → Hugs | Living well, love in action. Builds to emotional climax. |
| **Bridge 2** | *"Και τότε κατάλαβα..."* | Revelation: "Life is love, love is life." The climax. |
| **Final Chorus** | Full chorus with optional harmony | Emotional release, resolution. |
| **Outro** (Spoken) | *"Μία ζωή... μία χαρά... Με αγάπη... Ελλάδα..."* | Gentle, warm fade-out. |

## 4. Musical Specifications

| Element | Specification | Reason |
|---------|---------------|--------|
| **Key** | **G Major** | Warm, bright, accessible. Fits both bouzouki and guitar. |
| **Time Signature (Verses)** | **6/8** | Folk dance feel (kalamatianos). Gives a gentle sway. |
| **Time Signature (Chorus)** | **4/4** | Anthemic, sing-along. Provides a "solid" anchor. |
| **Tempo** | **92 BPM** | Moderate. Fast enough to be lively, slow enough to learn. |
| **Melodic Range** | **5 notes (D–A)** | Narrow pentatonic range. Accessible to children, adults, and non-singers. Prevents vocal fatigue. |
| **Style** | Greek Folk / Laïkó | Warm, rustic, not overly produced. |
| **Instrumentation** | Bouzouki, Acoustic Guitar, Light Percussion (Tambourine / Defi), optional Children's Choir in Choruses | Authentic Greek sound. Bouzouki is essential. Choir adds "school" warmth. |
| **Accents** | Rhythmic emphasis on beats 1 & 4 (in 6/8) | Natural Greek dance rhythm. |

## 5. Technical Notes for Singers (Meter & Diction)

### Syllable Count per Line (Average)

| Section | Average Syllables | Breath Recommendation |
|---------|-------------------|-----------------------|
| Chorus | 16-17 | Breath after line 2. |
| Verses 1-6 | 8-10 | Natural breath at line breaks. |
| Verse 7 (Line 1-2) | 13 | **Breath after the verb** (περπάτησα / γεμάτες). |
| Verses 8-13 | 10-12 | Natural breath at line breaks. |
| Verse 14 (Line 1-2) | 12 | Breath after the verb (είδα / ζεστές). |
| Bridge 1 | 9-14 | Slow down. Deliberate pauses at commas. |
| Bridge 2 | 9-17 | Build intensity. Breath between "όλα" phrases (final line). |

### Pronunciation Guide for Tricky Words

| Word | Greek | Phonetic (English) | Note |
|------|-------|-------------------|------|
| γνώρισα | γνώ-ρι-σα | **NYO**-ree-sa | The "γν" is like "ny" (palatal). Practice slowly. |
| ανθρώπους | αν-θρώ-πους | an-**THRO**-pous | Soft "th" (like in "this"). |
| δεκατέσσερις | δε-κα-τέσ-σε-ρις | de-ka-**TES**-se-ris | Stress on **TES**. Double "σ" is slightly held. |
| ειλικρινείς | ει-λι-κρι-νείς | ee-lee-kree-**NEES** | Stress on final. |
| μπουζούκι | μπου-ζού-κι | boo-**ZOO**-kee | "μπ" = "b". Soft "z" sound. |
| φιλότιμο | φι-λό-τι-μο | fee-**LO**-tee-mo | Stress on **LO**. |
| ευλογίες | ευ-λο-γί-ες | ev-lo-**YEE**-es | "ευ" = "ev". Stress on **YEE**. |

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid

1.  **Rushing the Cumulative List:** The listing part (e.g., "δύο θάλασσες, τρία νησιά...") should feel like a gentle roll-call, not a sprint. Use a melodic pattern (ascending or descending) to make it musical.
2.  **Misplacing Stress:** Greek is stress-timed. Ensure the singer emphasizes the accented syllables above all else.
3.  **Double Consonants:** In words like *Σάββατο* (Σάβ-βα-το) and *παππούδες* (παπ-πού-δες), the double consonant is held slightly longer. This is crucial for meaning and natural sound.
4.  **The "γει" Cluster:** *γειτονιές* is pronounced "yi-to-NYES" – the "γ" before "ε"/"ι" sounds like "y".

## 7. Production Notes & Reference

### Suggested Vibe / Reference Tracks

- **Melodic Vibe:** Traditional Greek folk songs like *"Τα Παιδιά του Πειραιά"* (Never on Sunday) – warm, slow *hasapiko* feel.
- **Cumulative Style:** *"The Twelve Days of Christmas"* (for the building structure) but with a Greek folk rhythm.
- **Choir Feel:** Children's choir in the choruses (like *"We Are the World"* style unity, but distinctly Greek).

### Recording Setup Suggestions

1.  **Lead Vocal:** Warm, not overly polished. Folk aesthetic. A male or female voice with a "kitchen table" warmth.
2.  **Backing Vocals:** A small children's choir (4-8 kids) for all choruses. Adds joy and educational feel.
3.  **Instruments:** Bouzouki should be prominent. Acoustic guitar strumming. Light percussion (tambourine, defi) to keep the 6/8 sway. Avoid synthesizers – keep it organic.
4.  **Spoken Parts (Intro/Outro):** Recorded by the lead singer, with a smile. Should feel like they're inviting you into their home.

### Mixing Notes

- **Chorus:** Wider mix, more reverb, choir louder.
- **Verses:** More intimate, bouzouki and lead vocal forward.
- **Bridge 1:** Drop the percussion. Just guitar and voice. Create a "hush".
- **Bridge 2:** Build intensity. Bring in all instruments. Crescendo to the final chorus.

## 9. Cultural & Life-Lesson Guide (For Performers & Marketers)

**Understanding the "Smell" of Faith/Wisdom without being religious:**

This song is designed to feel like a warm Greek grandmother's wisdom. It shouldn't sound preachy. Here is the subtext for the performers to channel:

| Lyric | Subtext / Life Lesson |
|-------|-----------------------|
| "Μία είναι η ζωή μας" | Life is precious – you only get one. |
| "Με φιλότιμο" | Live with honour, integrity, and duty to others. |
| "Όλα είναι δώρα" | Gratitude for the basic things we didn't earn. |
| "Μετρώ τις ευλογίες μου" | Counting blessings (a universal spiritual practice). |
| "Σταμάτα μια στιγμή" | Mindfulness / "Be still." |
| "Η ζωή είναι αγάπη" | The ultimate truth – love is the answer. |

**Performance Note:** Sing it with a smile. Let the joy be genuine. If the singer believes in the warmth, the audience will feel it. Do not over-sell the "wisdom" – let it sit naturally in the melody.
