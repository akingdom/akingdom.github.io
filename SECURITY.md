> **⚠️ Before you click – Don't Panic!**  
> 
> That page looks very technical, but **you don't need to be a developer** to use it. Here’s what to do:  
> 
> - **Title** – Just give it a short name (e.g., *"Private feedback"* or *"Concern about [topic]"*).  
> - **Description** – Write your message in plain English. **Ignore all the boxes below** (*Impact, Patches, Ecosystem, Severity, CWE*) – leave them blank, I don't need them.  
> - **Account required** – You’ll need a free GitHub account to submit. I know signing up is a hassle – I only ask because it keeps out bots and ensures messages are genuine. I won’t share your info.  
> 
> After you submit, it creates a private draft that **only I can see**.
